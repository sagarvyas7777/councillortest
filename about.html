About

Meet me.

Rooted in Ward 11

I have proudly called the Manor Park neighborhood home for over four years. It’s where my wife and I are raising our two young daughters, and where I am deeply invested in building a safe, vibrant future for every family on the block.

A Practical, Problem-Solving Background

Effective local government requires a deep understanding of how things are built, how they function, and how communities grow. I bring a unique blend of technical expertise and local market insight to City Hall:

Engineering & Technical Expertise: As a trained Engineer (B.Eng) and Licensed Millwright, I understand the value of preventative maintenance, structural accountability, and finding practical, data-driven solutions to complex infrastructure challenges.

Local Housing & Community Insight: As a professional Real Estate Salesperson, I work face-to-face with residents every day, giving me a clear, firsthand understanding of the economic realities, housing needs, and everyday challenges facing London families.

Why Run for Ward 11 Council?

Our community deserves a councillor who shows up—not just during election season, but at your doors, at neighborhood meetings, and consistently at City Hall.

I am running a collaborative, non-partisan campaign focused on delivering real results for our neighborhood. My key priorities include:

Infrastructure Accountability: Ensuring public funds are invested wisely in robust, long-term infrastructure upgrades that keep our city moving.

Public Safety: Working alongside community members to build safer streets and secure neighborhoods for our children.

Transparent Leadership: Listening first, deciding second, and always reporting back honestly to the residents of Ward 11.

Outside of Work

When I am not advocating for the community or helping clients, you’ll find me spending quality time with my wife and daughters, supporting local independent businesses, or catching up with neighbors at community events like Sunfest, multicultural and the Gathering on the Green in Wortley Village.
