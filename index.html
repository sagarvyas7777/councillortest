My campaign is community-first.

Our priorities

Five commitments for Ward 11

Better & Functional Roads

Better planning for road construction, with clear timelines so residents know when work is expected to begin. I also want to explore AI-powered pothole detection using City service vehicles to identify road problems in real time and help get them addressed faster.

Vacancy Tax on Empty Homes & Apartments

Introduce a vacancy tax on qualifying empty homes and apartments to encourage unused housing to return to the market. Increasing available rental supply could help ease pressure on the rental market and improve housing availability for Londoners.

Making London More Affordable

Launch a City's Grocery Store initiative, operated by the City of London with professional partners, focused on essential groceries at lower prices. The goal is to help low-income families save on everyday necessities and keep more money in their pockets.

Predictable Taxes & Fiscal Discipline

Keep annual property-tax increases within a predictable 3-4% range, while maintaining essential infrastructure and accounting for inflation. I will push for greater financial discipline in municipal spending while ensuring London properly maintains its roads, water lines and sewer systems.

Environment & Climate Resilience

Protect London's Natural Heritage System, urban tree canopy, green spaces and Environmentally Significant Areas (ESAs). Invest in green infrastructure, flood mitigation and sustainable planning to help build a more climate-resilient city while respecting neighbourhood concerns.

Safe Parks & Public Spaces

Work toward parks, sidewalks, bike lanes and public spaces that everyone can safely enjoy. Support a coordinated approach to harmful public drug use that combines outreach, access to treatment and enforcement of public-safety rules, while keeping the needs of neighbourhoods at the centre.
